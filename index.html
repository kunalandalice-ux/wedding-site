01 — How We Met

The Headhunt

If you ask Alice, she didn't just "find" Kunal on Hinge, she practically headhunted him. After making the first move... and the second... and the third... she finally landed an interview over dinner. Kunal, ever the strategist, decided to capitalise on the government's "Eat Out to Help Out" scheme for their first date at Kimchi in London. Because nothing says "I might be the one" quite like a 50% discount and a state-subsidised bibimbap.

The First Impression

The chemistry was instant, even if the coordination wasn't. Alice was so "head over heels" (her words) that she immediately redecorated the table with a full glass of red wine. Not to be outdone in the nerves department, Kunal responded by monologuing at a record-breaking speed that would have made a professional auctioneer sweat. We realised then that while the food was half-price, the company was priceless.

The "Hangry" Revelation

Date two was the real test: a romantic, six-hour trek through a ghost-town London where every single restaurant was shuttered. It was here that Kunal was introduced to the legend of 'Hangry Alice.' As they wandered past closed kitchens, he learned a vital life lesson: the key to Alice's heart—and his own physical safety—is keeping her fed.

The World Tour

That first walk turned into a six-year trek across the globe. From National Park road trips in the US & Canada, month-long adventures in Thailand, whale watching in the Azores, abusing all-inclusive alcohol in Greece, we've eaten and drank our way through so much of the world. We've learned that whether we're huddled over street food at a plastic table in Bangkok or sitting in a restaurant where we have to nod solemnly at a wine cork and pretend we can taste "notes of leather," as long as we're together (and Alice has been fed), life is perfect.

The Result

Six years, several thousand miles, and (mostly) zero spilled drinks later, we are finally trading those nervous first dates for a lifetime of shared seats at the table. Kunal has learned the golden rule of a happy life — always keep his woman fed — and Alice has learned the art of the "quiet lead," letting her man believe he's firmly in charge.

(Please note: For the safety of everyone at the wedding, wine glasses should still be kept at a safe distance from Alice at all times.)

02 — The Proposal

The Secret

Six months before the flight to Thailand, Kunal hand-designed the ring. What followed was a half-year game of high-stakes hide-and-seek. The ring lived in the kitchen, the office, the underwear drawer and eventually under the bed as Kunal moved it weekly to stay one step ahead of Alice's intuition.

The Camera Bag Gambit

Once in Thailand, the stress reached new heights. For an entire month, Kunal carried his camera bag like it was a holy relic. From the get go of the trip, a suspicious domestic battle broke out: Kunal insisted his "camera equipment" was far more valuable than Alice's actual jewellery and demanded the safe for himself. Alice was confused; Kunal was sweating.

Plan A (The Monkey Business)

The original plan was a romantic treehouse in the jungle, overlooking a serene river. However, between a troop of territorial monkeys and some fellow guests who were... ahem... enjoying the jungle scenery a bit too explicitly, Kunal decided the vibe was thoroughly compromised. Plan A was abandoned in favour of the final stop.

The Question (Eventually)

At a floating water villa surrounded by the howling of gibbons and open water, Kunal finally found his moment. He had spent months perfecting a speech that would have brought a tear to a statue's eye. But as he knelt, the "First Date Nerves" returned with a vengeance. After five minutes of eloquent waffling, Alice had to gently interrupt the monologue to remind him of a minor detail: he hadn't actually asked the question yet.

The Yes

He asked, she said yes, and the ring, finally out of the camera bag, found its permanent home!

Getting Here
